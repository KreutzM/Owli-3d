## Closed findings and Goal criteria

- **V18-01 closed / F-01 resolved:** Preview01 had near-vertical thin warm trim.
  Preview02/run02 moved roots outward/up and tips inward while slightly broadening
  the localized orange/gold core. Final run03 front/3/4 and the chest board retain
  the resulting tapered diagonal contours, cream transitions and cool outer
  edges. This corrects actual distribution and group placement rather than
  merely changing the hex color on the old solid orange leaves.
- **V18-02 closed:** Preview01's shallow crown hub was almost invisible.
  The revised lower position, increased radius and rounder dome recover the
  central network identity in front, with a naturally smaller visible trace
  in 3/4. The graph stays seated rather than returning to a floating antenna.
- **Actual assigned finishing:** Fresh-loaded mesh slots show all 95 non-eye
  meshes using MAT_* production roles. Visible navy/blue/cyan surfaces remain
  soft and nonmetallic; cream stays readable; orange keratin and dark claws
  have controlled highlights. Perch metal and thin cyan accents remain secondary.
  Exact shader numerics and sRGB-to-linear semantics are independently assessed
  by the technical review; image appearance alone cannot prove those numerics.
- **Reference and preservation scope:** Four unchanged cameras expose front,
  profile, back and 3/4. No general concept, flight geometry or individual-feather
  simulation was introduced. The inherited broad silhouette and perched pose
  remain readable; technical proofs retain the separate function/grip requirements.

## Practical limits and named remaining work

The small network graph foreshortens strongly along the curved crown in
profile/3/4 and is clearest from front. This is documented rather than hidden
through camera changes. It is a simplified editable interpretation of 00/08,
with one forehead motif and restrained cyan emission.

The geometry uses a few broad groups over clean primary surfaces rather than
the illustrated references' dense individual feathers. Cream tiers remain
visibly simplified, and smooth primary areas remain exposed. This review does
not claim a pixel-identical beauty render or a new full-character shape freeze.

Eye optical depth, iris gradient/network/catchlights and the remaining F-03
visual mismatch belong to #19. F-02's missing nostril relief remains the
separate #40 geometry task. Their absence is acknowledged and does not block
the bounded #18 scope. #40 must remain open until all its findings are delivered
together. Full combined topology, rig and action QA remains #20–24.

This reviewer did not independently rerun the entire continuous collision,
pose, 3+1 connectivity or eight claw-distance sweep. Actual images cannot prove
those numerical properties. The commissioned technical review owns those probes.
This review independently confirms actual references/images, fresh-open mesh
inventory, assigned slots, unchanged studio and exact neutral pixel buffers.
