# Owli — Character Bible

## Role

Owli is the small Tech-Eule and brand mascot of Owli-AI.

The character should communicate:
- intelligence;
- attentiveness;
- curiosity;
- calm confidence;
- friendliness;
- technological competence without looking robotic.

## Visual direction

The central design idea is:

> **A living stylized owl with digital intelligence — not a robot shaped like an owl.**

Owli should remain immediately recognizable from the original 2D logo.

## Silhouette

- compact perched owl;
- head visually dominant but not infantile;
- broad expressive facial area;
- body tapers toward tail;
- folded wings create clean side silhouettes;
- pointed feather-ear/brow tufts define the upper silhouette;
- feet visibly and believably grip the perch.

## Face

- very large glossy eyes;
- blue-to-cyan iris;
- subtle network motif inside the eye;
- cream/white facial mask;
- orange beak;
- forehead network symbol integrated into feather parting;
- neutral expression should read attentive and kind.

## Body

- stylized layered plumage;
- deep navy base;
- royal blue and cyan accents;
- white/cream chest center;
- limited warm orange accent feathers;
- tail compact and symmetrical at rest.

## Feet

Each foot has:
- 3 forward toes/claws;
- 1 rear toe/claw.

The rear claw must wrap behind the perch and be visible in side/3/4 validation where appropriate.

Feet are stylized but mechanically believable for a perched owl.

## Perch

V1 uses a simple modern tech perch:
- cylindrical brushed-metal bar;
- rounded ends;
- restrained cyan light rings;
- stable centered support/base;
- visually secondary to Owli.

## Animation personality

Owli should gain most of her personality through:
- eye direction;
- blinks;
- subtle pupil/iris emphasis;
- head tilt;
- small body lean;
- beak movement;
- restrained wing gestures.

Avoid overactive cartoon motion.
